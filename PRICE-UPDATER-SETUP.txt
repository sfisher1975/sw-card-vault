SW CARD VAULT v1.0 — ONE-BUTTON PRICE UPDATER

The app uses a small Cloudflare Worker because a GitHub Pages browser app cannot reliably request PriceCharting pages directly.

CURRENT WORKER
Use price-worker.js from this release in your existing Cloudflare Worker and deploy it.

APP SETUP
1. Open SW Card Vault.
2. Open More / Tools.
3. Paste your deployed workers.dev URL into One-button Price Updater.
4. Tap Save updater URL.
5. Reload the app once.
6. Open a card and tap Update Prices.

The updater uses that card's exact PriceCharting URL from prices-v1.0.json and reads the Full Price Guide section.
It returns Raw, Grade 7, Grade 8, and Grade 9 plus the update date.
